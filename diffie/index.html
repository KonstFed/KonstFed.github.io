DiffIE: Diffusion-based Open Information Extraction

Konstantin Fedorov, Valentin Malykh · EMNLP 2026 Oral

One sentence usually states several facts. DiffIE treats Open Information Extraction as discrete diffusion over per-token role tags: many independent reverse-diffusion trajectories produce candidate triplets, which are clustered into the final set. The number of extractions is chosen at inference time, not fixed in training.
